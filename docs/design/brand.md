# Brand — Pulseboard

## Identity

Pulseboard is the **open-source Android network monitor** for power users, security professionals, and anyone who wants to know what their phone is actually doing on the network.

## Story

Modern Android phones make hundreds of network requests per minute — most of them silently, in the background. Pulseboard makes that invisible traffic visible: per-app, per-connection, per-packet. Built OSS so security-conscious users can audit the source themselves.

## Voice

- **Direct, technically credible** — power users and security pros, not casual consumers
- **Privacy-respectful** — Pulseboard never sends data off-device; emphasize that
- **Open-source proud** — call out OSS, sponsorship model, contributor onboarding

### Do
- "Watch every packet. On your terms."
- "Open source. On-device. No cloud."
- "Built for power users. Sponsored by people who care."

### Don't
- Marketing fluff ("AI-powered", "intelligent insights")
- Threat-fearmongering ("hackers everywhere!")
- Locked-down feature gating (it's OSS — features are free)

## Audience

- **Primary**: Android power users, indie hackers, security-conscious developers
- **Secondary**: mobile security researchers, pentest students
- **Tertiary**: privacy-focused journalists / activists who need network audit on travel-burner phones

## Sponsor-ready

The product is OSS; revenue comes from sponsorship. The landing surfaces:
- GitHub Sponsors button (primary CTA after Play Store install)
- Open Collective backer page link
- Tier benefits: $5/mo backers get logo on landing, $25/mo get priority issue triage, $100/mo get a feature-vote seat

## Domain

`pulseboard.build` (preferred — `.build` TLD signals dev-tool ethos). Backup: `pulseboard.dev` or `pulseboard.app`.
